# Vertical slice — Unexpected Guest

An original fictional hotel infiltration mission. Third-person shoulder camera, one agent, movement and exploration first. Later: fictional objective, extraction, perception, stealth, combat, dialogue, reputation and progression. Safehouse and campaign follow the playable slice.

Visual direction: grounded contemporary espionage, dark teal and warm amber, hotel stone and wood, clear silhouettes, restrained HUD. Placeholder geometry is not final art. Independent project from Tactical; revisit overlap after the slice.
